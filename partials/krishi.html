Agriculture / Case study

Krishi Vikash

Audience / context
Regional and agriculture-focused audiences

Purpose / presence

These posts strengthen Krishi Vikas’s social presence by connecting the brand with culturally meaningful occasions relevant to its audience. The Bhai Dooj post uses a warm rural setting, while the Eid creative brings agricultural imagery into the festive greeting, helping the brand remain visible without making the communication overtly promotional.

Thought behind it

The creative approach was to make each festival feel natural to Krishi Vikas rather than using generic greetings. Bhai Dooj combines the occasion with a farming backdrop and familiar rural setting, while the Eid post cleverly forms the crescent from cultivated land, visually connecting celebration with the brand’s agricultural identity.
