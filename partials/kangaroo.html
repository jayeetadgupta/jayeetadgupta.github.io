Education / Case study

Kangaroo Kids

Audience / context
Parents · approx. 25–45

Purpose / presence

These posts help Kangaroo Kids maintain a warm, child-focused social presence by balancing festive communication with real classroom experiences. The Ganesh Puja post connects a cultural occasion with playful childhood imagery, while the artist-visit post highlights creative learning and gives parents a glimpse into everyday activities beyond academics.

Thought behind it

The approach was to keep children at the centre of both communications. The festive post uses colourful, playful visuals to make Ganesh Puja more relatable to young learners, while the artist session uses real activity-based content to showcase imagination, colours, self-expression, and the school’s hands-on learning environment.
