Automotive / Case study

Toyota Dealerships

Audience / context
Working professionals, business owners and prospective buyers · approx. 23–55

Purpose / presence

The posts were designed to keep Toyota’s social presence relevant beyond conventional vehicle promotions. One uses a current social-media expression to spotlight the Rumion in a playful, attention-grabbing way, while the Mahasaptami creative connects the Glanza with a culturally significant festive moment and regional audience.

Thought behind it

The approach was to give the brand a voice that changes with the context. For the Rumion, “So Goated” turns an online phrase into a memorable product hook. For Mahasaptami, Bengali copy and festive visual elements create regional familiarity while keeping the Toyota vehicle naturally integrated into the communication.
