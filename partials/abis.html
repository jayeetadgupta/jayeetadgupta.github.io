Food / Case study

Abis Laziz

Audience / context
Consumers looking for convenient preserved chicken products

Purpose / presence

These posts position Abis Laziz as both promotional and useful in everyday food planning. The monsoon campaign creates season-led purchase urgency through a visible discount and “Order Now” message, while the protein-focused post presents fresh chicken as a convenient choice for regular meals and daily routines.

Thought behind it

The approach combines timely context with clear product action. The monsoon creative links rainy-weather food cravings with a sale to encourage immediate orders, while the second post shifts from discount-led communication to a practical protein message, using the online store interface to reinforce easy product discovery and ordering.
